Sources & method

Descriptions first. No invented routes.

These own-words guides are based on the official PlayStation list as identified by the supplied starter. The 55 names, descriptions, and grades are preserved exactly. We have not independently authenticated that list.

Each trophy names PlayStation official trophy list and PSNProfiles trophy list as reference labels. They are not claims that external pages were checked. No unverified location, chapter, count, or strategy is presented as a fact.

Pre-release means provisional

Unconfirmed mechanics and story timing are explicitly flagged. Counts come only from numbers in descriptions; chapter numbers, wanted levels, and percentages are contextual targets, not cumulative tasks. Counters are manual and do not automatically award trophies.

Missability: evidence before warnings

A trophy is flagged only if its description implies a one-time or story-window opportunity. None of the supplied descriptions establishes one. That does not confirm that everything is obtainable after the story. Stage 2 is a precautionary checkpoint.

Your data stays with you

No accounts, analytics, or external requests. Progress is stored in this browser. Export a backup before clearing browser data. Import replaces current progress after validating the file.
